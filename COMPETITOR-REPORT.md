## Executive Summary

The Android dashcam app market is fragmented and currently experiencing a consolidation moment. AutoBoy — the long-dominant player with 10+ million downloads — was removed from the Play Store in September 2024, leaving a significant gap among established power users. Meanwhile, Drive Recorder's move to $14.99/week subscription pricing has triggered visible user backlash, creating a direct acquisition channel for alternatives.

Phone Dashcam (DriveSight) holds a genuinely differentiated position: it combines loop recording with a 336,000+ US speed camera and radar alert database that no pure-app competitor matches. Combined with a one-time $9.99 Pro pricing model and YOLOv8 AI object detection, the product has multiple moats that are defensible in the near term.

The primary competitive threats are Droid Dashcam (2.2M downloads, strong app store presence, aggressive ASO) and Roadly (closest feature parity including speed camera alerts, though European-centric). DailyRoads Voyager holds a loyalty moat through sheer install age but has not updated meaningfully in years.

The biggest strategic opportunity is capturing disaffected Drive Recorder users via targeted "alternative" positioning, while doubling down on the US-focused camera alert database as the primary differentiator that makes Phone Dashcam unique in the category.

Top 3 recommendations: (1) Create `/vs/droid-dashcam` and `/vs/drive-recorder` landing pages targeting bottom-of-funnel switchers, (2) Build the camera alert count into every headline and ad creative, (3) Publish a direct comparison table on the homepage that forces competitors to respond on your terms.
