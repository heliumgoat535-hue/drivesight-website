DriveSight

At a Glance

DriveSight’s marketing materials state a built-in offline database of over 336,000 camera locations in the US. That database powers live speed, red light, and police alerts even when the phone has no cellular connection. The app is a free Android dash cam with optional Pro features for advanced detection and cloud backup.

Core Features

DriveSight records continuous loop video with adjustable quality and saves crash clips automatically to prevent overwrite. It runs AI object detection for vehicles, pedestrians, and hazards while showing detections on a live map overlay and issuing police and ALPR alerts. Parking mode uses motion detection with optional auto-upload to Google Drive, and the app supports external USB rear cameras and trip history customization.

Key Differentiator

The defining feature is on-device AI combined with the offline camera database, so alerts and object detection work without sending video to a remote server. That architecture keeps most processing local to the phone and reduces reliance on mobile data while driving. For drivers who need alerts without constant connectivity this point matters more than cloud-only systems.

Pros

DriveSight turns an Android phone into a capable dash cam with features that usually require separate hardware. The free tier removes upfront cost for basic recording and parking mode while the Pro subscription unlocks AI detection and police alerts. Support for external USB rear cameras and automatic crash save makes the app practical for commuters, delivery drivers, and ride-share operators who want evidence-grade footage.

Cons

  • Reliability depends on the phone being mounted and functioning properly; crashes, forgotten mounting, or phone overheating can cause missed recordings.

Who It’s For

Android drivers who want a low-cost alternative to dedicated dash cam hardware and who already own a compatible phone. People who park on the street or use their vehicle for work will find parking mode and crash save valuable. Those who need 24 hour monitoring should plan for continuous power and a vented mount to avoid heat issues.

Unique Value Proposition

On-device video processing plus local storage means DriveSight preserves privacy by default while offering optional cloud backup to Google Drive. That workflow lets you keep footage on the phone for routine drives and push critical clips to the cloud for safekeeping. For buyers comparing options, this reduces ongoing cloud storage needs and keeps most data under your control.

Real World Use Case

A delivery driver mounts an old Android phone in the windshield and enables parking mode overnight. DriveSight records drives, warns about nearby speed cameras, and saves a crash clip automatically after an impact. Important footage uploads to Google Drive when the driver connects to Wi Fi, simplifying claims and evidence sharing.

Pricing

DriveSight is free to download with core recording and parking features available at no cost. The Pro subscription costs $4.99 per month or $29.99 per year and unlocks AI detection, police alerts, and additional conveniences. The pricing model lets you test basic functions before committing to the subscription.
