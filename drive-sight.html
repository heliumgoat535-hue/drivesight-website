How to use DriveSight footage for insurance claims

Dashcam footage is only useful in a claim if it is timestamped, GPS-tagged, and stored before it loops over. DriveSight handles all three automatically. The G-sensor detects an impact and locks the clip instantly, preventing it from being overwritten. GPS and timestamp overlays appear directly on the video frame, which insurers and attorneys can read without any additional software. After an incident, open the clips screen, export the locked file, and upload it to Google Drive for a permanent off-device copy. Most US insurers accept MP4 files with embedded metadata. If the other driver disputes fault, a 4K front clip with GPS data is far stronger evidence than a witness statement alone. For parked-car incidents, the parking surveillance mode captures motion-triggered clips with timestamps even when you are not in the vehicle.
