GridWatch Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 25, 2026

GridWatch ("we", "our", "the app") is a public safety awareness application developed by CyberLab Automation. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

1. Information We Collect

Location Data: GridWatch uses your device's GPS to show nearby crime data, cameras, community reports, and scanner feeds. Location is used only for map positioning and proximity alerts. We do not track your location history on our servers.

Community Reports: When you submit a report, we store the report type, description, your approximate location, timestamp, and a randomly generated nickname. Reports are stored in Google Firebase Firestore and are visible to other users nearby.

Photos: If you attach a photo to a report, it is uploaded to Firebase Storage and linked to your report. Photos are visible to other users who view your report.

Chat Messages: Messages posted in the geo chat feed are stored in Firebase Firestore with your nickname, approximate location, and timestamp. Messages are visible to nearby users.

Anonymous Authentication: We use Firebase Anonymous Authentication. No email, password, or personal identity is required. A random anonymous user ID is assigned to your device.

Bluetooth Data: The tracker radar feature scans for nearby Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) devices to detect potential tracking devices (AirTags, Tile, etc.). Scan results are stored locally on your device only and are never uploaded.

Network Scan Data: Wi-Fi network scan results are stored locally on your device only and are never uploaded.

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

  • Your real name, email address, or phone number
  • Contacts, call logs, or SMS messages
  • Browsing history
  • Files or documents on your device
  • Continuous background location tracking

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Display nearby crime data, cameras, and community reports on the map
  • Enable community reporting and geo-based chat
  • Provide proximity alerts while driving
  • Show relevant police scanner feeds for your area
  • Calculate neighborhood safety scores
  • Display advertisements via Google AdMob

4. Third-Party Services

GridWatch uses the following third-party services, each with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Storage) — Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob (Advertising) — Privacy Policy
  • Google Play Services (Location) — Privacy Policy
  • OpenStreetMap / CartoDB (Map tiles) — Privacy Policy
  • Broadcastify (Scanner audio streams) — Privacy Policy

5. Data Retention

  • Community reports auto-expire after 2-48 hours depending on type
  • Chat messages are retained for 24 hours
  • Local device data (tracker scans, alerts) can be cleared in Settings
  • Firebase anonymous accounts may be automatically cleaned up by Google after extended inactivity

6. Children's Privacy

GridWatch is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights

You can:

  • Clear all local data from within the app's Settings screen
  • Uninstall the app to remove all local data
  • Request deletion of your community reports by contacting us

8. Advertising

GridWatch displays ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers and usage data to serve personalized ads. You can opt out of personalized ads in your device's Google settings.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective Date" above.
