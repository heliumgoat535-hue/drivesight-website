Where Droid Dashcam Wins

Droid Dashcam has a larger install base and more reviews, which is a legitimate signal. More users means more edge cases tested, more bugs caught, and a broader range of device configurations verified to work. If you've heard recommendations from other drivers or have been using it without issues, that's real.

Droid Dashcam's UI is also praised consistently for being clean and not overwhelming. If you want to install an app, point it at the road, and never look at settings again — Droid Dashcam delivers that.

It's also about $5 cheaper for the Pro upgrade.

Where DriveSight Wins

Everything beyond basic recording.

Droid Dashcam does not have a camera alert database. It does not alert you to speed cameras, red light cameras, Flock Safety ALPR readers, or police speed traps. DriveSight has 336,000+ of these locations mapped across the United States, and it warns you as you approach them with the camera type, street name, and distance. This feature alone is worth the $4.99 difference in Pro pricing.

DriveSight also includes YOLOv8 AI object detection, which identifies pedestrians, cyclists, and vehicles in real time. And it integrates with Android Auto, letting you monitor the dashcam feed and camera alerts on your car's head unit — a feature no other comparable app offers.

Is DriveSight better than Droid Dashcam?

DriveSight has more features — 336K+ camera alerts, AI object detection, Android Auto, and Google Drive backup. Droid Dashcam has a larger install base and slightly higher community-driven rating. Both record well. For drivers who want alerts and AI detection, DriveSight is the better choice.

Does Droid Dashcam have speed camera alerts?

No. Droid Dashcam does not include speed camera alerts, police trap warnings, or any camera alert database. DriveSight Pro includes 336,000+ US camera locations.

Can I have both apps installed?

Yes, but two dashcam apps cannot record simultaneously — only one can use the camera at a time. You'd need to choose one as your active dashcam.
