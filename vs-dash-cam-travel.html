The Background Recording Difference

This is worth spending a moment on because it is the most common failure point for dashcam apps that get recommended without testing.

Android aggressively kills background apps to conserve battery. A dashcam app that does not hold a foreground service with a persistent notification can be terminated by the OS at any point — during a long drive, when you switch to maps, or when your phone's memory runs low. When that happens, you have a gap in your recording that you will only discover after the fact.

DriveSight holds a foreground service with an always-visible notification throughout every recording session. The Android system treats it as a running service, not a background task. It cannot be killed mid-drive. Every minute of your drive is on record.
