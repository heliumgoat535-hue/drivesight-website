    Droid Dashcam

    Best for: free, no-fuss recording with GPS details written into the video.

    Droid Dashcam is the app XDA, MakeUseOf and How-To Geek each wrote about in 2026. It loops, records in the background, locks a clip when the phone's G-sensor detects an impact, and writes the time, address, GPS coordinates, speed and your plate number into the video. Why isn't AutoGuard Dash Cam ranked?

AutoGuard Dash Cam - Blackbox (com.hovans.autoguard, developer Ben Yoo) is still listed on Google Play, but it has not been updated since August 14, 2023, and AppBrain and APKPure list it as needing Android 12 or later. That update came before Android 15 existed, so the app cannot meet Google Play's rule that apps target Android 15 by August 31, 2026 (November 1, 2026 with an extension). Under that rule, Google Play stops offering an app to new users whose phones run a newer Android version than the app targets, so new users on Android 15 and 16 phones lose access by November 1, 2026 at the latest. Phones on Android 11 or older can't install it either. People who already have it keep it. We have not confirmed this on a phone, so if Play still offers Install on yours, it is available to you.

AutoGuard loops, records speed, GPS and the nearest address, traces the drive on a map and uploads to YouTube with location captions. Background recording and Bluetooth auto-start need its $2.99 Pro upgrade, which also removes the ads.

Pros and cons of using a phone as a dash cam

Pros

  • Cheap: every app above is free to start, and a spare phone may already be in a drawer.
  • Built-in hardware: a phone already has GPS, a good camera, a screen and a data connection, so speed, location and cloud upload are easy to add.
  • Software can add more: apps can add things most dash cams lack, such as camera and police alerts or object detection.
  • Easy to review: clips can be watched, trimmed and shared from the phone itself.

Cons

  • Heat: a phone's lithium battery does not like a hot windshield. Many dedicated dash cams use a supercapacitor instead, which copes with heat better.
  • Starting with the car: a dash cam wired to the car starts by itself. A phone only does if the app has an auto-start option and you set it up.
  • It ties up a phone: your everyday phone is busy while you drive, and a phone left on the dashboard can be stolen.
  • Extra parts: you need a steady mount and a car charger.

Questions drivers ask about dash cam apps

Can I use my phone as a dash cam?

Yes. An Android phone works as a dash cam with an app such as DriveSight Dash Cam, Droid Dashcam or Dash Cam Travel. Mount it on the windshield with the rear camera facing the road, keep it on a car charger, and turn on loop recording so the oldest footage is replaced automatically. A spare phone that stays in the car works best, because your everyday phone stays free.

What is the best free dash cam app for Android?

Droid Dashcam is the best free dash cam app for Android for plain recording. It records in the background, loops, locks clips on impact and writes GPS speed and location into the video, with ads. DriveSight Dash Cam is free to record with, has no ads and keeps severe weather warnings free, but its AI detection, police and camera alerts, parking mode and impact clips need Pro after a short free run.

Do dash cam apps work in the background?

Most do. DriveSight Dash Cam, Droid Dashcam, Driver, Dash Cam Travel and Drive Recorder keep recording while you use maps or music; AutoGuard needs its paid Pro upgrade for that. Some phones still stop background apps to save battery, which Droid Dashcam's own FAQ says happens more on some Chinese brands than on Samsung and Pixel phones. Allowing the app unrestricted battery use in Android settings usually fixes it.

Which dash cam app has police and speed camera alerts?

Of the apps compared here, DriveSight Dash Cam is the only one whose listing includes police and camera alerts. With Pro, it alerts for community-reported police locations, speed cameras, license-plate-reader cameras including Flock, and police and military aircraft that broadcast their position. Dash Cam Travel can show the speed limit on screen and Drive Recorder warns when you drive too fast, but neither lists camera or police alerts.

Which Android dash cam app has parking mode?

DriveSight Dash Cam is the only one of the apps compared here that describes a parking mode. In DriveSight Pro, parking mode records while the car is parked, flags a person who lingers for three seconds or walks toward the car, and lets you watch the camera live from a web browser and talk through the phone. The phone needs a power source to run it for long.

Do dash cam apps work without internet?

Recording does. These apps save video to the phone without a data connection, and DriveSight Dash Cam's loop recording, clip saving and on-device detection all work offline. Cloud backup needs a connection in every app. DriveSight's US camera, police and aircraft alerts and its weather warnings also load over mobile data as you drive; only its UK and France speed camera lists are built in.

Is a phone dash cam as good as a real dash cam?

For everyday driving, a recent phone records clear enough video to show what happened, and a dash cam app can add things most dash cams lack, such as camera alerts and object detection. A dedicated dash cam is built to sit on a hot windshield all day, often with a supercapacitor instead of a lithium battery, starts with the car and stays small.

Does a dash cam app drain the battery or overheat the phone?

Recording with the camera and screen on uses a lot of power, so keep the phone on a car charger while driving. Phones also get hot on a sunny windshield, and AI detection adds heat. DriveSight Dash Cam slows its detection when Android reports the phone is warming up and stops recording when the battery runs low. A vent mount and shade from the sun help.

Which dash cam apps back up video to the cloud?

Driver backs up trips to its own Driver Cloud, with two days of history on the free Basic plan and one year on Premium. DriveSight Dash Cam Pro uploads impact, photo and parking alert clips to your own Google Drive automatically, and other clips when you choose. Dash Cam Travel, Drive Recorder and AutoGuard can upload to YouTube. Droid Dashcam has no built-in backup, but its FAQ suggests syncing its recording folder with Google Photos or Drive.

Does DriveSight work on iPhone or with Android Auto?

No. DriveSight Dash Cam is Android-only and needs Android 8.0 or later. There is no iPhone version, and the version on Google Play in October 2026 does not include Android Auto. Of the apps compared here, Driver is the one that lists Android Auto support. DriveSight records with the phone's own cameras, or with a USB camera plugged into the phone in Pro.

DriveSight Pro costs $4.99 a month or $29.99 a year in the US Google Play store. New users also get every Pro feature for their first few drives before deciding. The free version has no ads, records at up to 720p, keeps up to 2 hours of loop footage and keeps severe weather warnings after the free drives end.
