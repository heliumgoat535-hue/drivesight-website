Features

Everything you need in a dash cam app

Recording is the easy part — every dash cam does it. DriveSight is built around the things a windshield camera physically can't do: telling you what's ahead of you, and showing you your car while you're not in it.

Live police alerts Pro

Community-reported police sightings appear on your map in real time, the moment another driver marks one. No dash cam on the market does this — a windshield camera can only tell you what already happened.

336,000+ camera alerts

Speed cameras, red-light cameras, Flock Safety ALPR readers and reported traps across the US. Proximity warnings call out the street name and distance, with sound and haptics — before you're in range, not after.

Aircraft alerts Pro

Police helicopters and surveillance aircraft tracked from live ADS-B feeds and surfaced on the same map. The other thing no windshield camera will ever tell you.

Severe weather alerts New

Tornado, severe thunderstorm and flash flood warnings from each country's official weather service, announced out loud when your GPS position is inside one — with an on-screen banner until the warning ends. Free for everyone, in the US, Canada, UK, Mexico, Brazil and 29 European countries.

Live remote viewing Pro

Stream your parked car to any web browser, with two-way audio. Check on it from your desk, your hotel, or the table you're sitting at — while it's still happening.

Parking mode Popular

24/7 security while you're away. Motion detection wakes the camera, the flash strobes to deter, and clips upload to Google Drive on their own. Ultra-low power on AMOLED screens.

AI object detection Pro

On-device AI draws live bounding boxes around people, cars, trucks, motorcycles and animals, and auto-saves clips when it sees something. Runs entirely on your phone — no cloud, no privacy trade.

Loop recording & crash detection

Continuous loop recording at 1080p, 720p or 480p with SD card support. Impact detection locks the clip the instant a crash registers and protects it from loop deletion.

Google Drive backup

Crash clips and parking events upload to your own Drive automatically, with smart filtering and a WiFi-only option. Your evidence survives a lost or stolen phone.

Traffic map New

Your dashcam counts the vehicles it sees and scores every trip in vehicles per mile. Roads glow on a crowd-sourced map built from anonymous data. See how it works →
