Parking Mode & Dog Mode

A Dashcam That Watches Your Car While You're Parked

Phone Dashcam's parking mode turns any Android phone into a 24/7 car security camera. Motion detection saves a clip and instantly uploads it to Google Drive — evidence preserved before anyone drives away. Dog Mode displays a message on screen while you're gone. Check a live camera feed from any device, anywhere.

Step 1
Enable Parking Mode

Tap the parking icon before you walk away. The screen dims to near-zero to save battery and motion detection arms after a short exit delay so your own footsteps don't trigger it.

Step 2
Dog Mode Stays On Screen

The screen shows a message for anyone who looks in. When motion is detected, the clip is saved and automatically uploaded to Google Drive — evidence is preserved and viewable before you're back.

Step 3
Watch From Anywhere

Open the web viewer on any phone or laptop. Switch between front and rear cameras, talk through the phone's speaker, or trigger the flash to deter someone approaching your car.
