SpeedSight Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 19, 2026

SpeedSight ("we", "our", "the app") is a GPS speedometer for Android developed by Cyberlab Automation. This policy explains what the app does with your data, which services it uses, and the choices you have.

1. Location

SpeedSight reads your phone's GPS to measure your speed. It reads your location only while the speedometer is running, and Android shows its notification the whole time. If you leave the app during a trip, it keeps running until the trip ends. It does not ask for Android's background location permission.

Your speed, position and trips are processed on your phone. Two features send your current latitude and longitude to our servers:

  • Posted speed limits (Pro): the app asks our speed-limit service for the limit on the road you are on. The service looks it up in OpenStreetMap data and keeps the answer for nearby drivers, filed under a rounded location of about 110 m for up to 12 hours.
  • Speed camera alerts (Pro): the app asks our camera service for the speed cameras within about 15 km of you, from OpenStreetMap data. UK cameras come from a list built into the app.

These requests carry no account, name, advertising ID or other identifier, and we do not store the locations they contain. Like any internet service, our servers see your IP address; it is held in memory for a few minutes to limit request rates and is not stored with your location. The services run on Google Cloud and query OpenStreetMap's Overpass API from our servers, not from your phone.

2. Trips

Trips (route points, distance, time, average and top speed) are saved in the app's storage on your phone and are not uploaded. When you export a trip as a GPX file, it goes only where you choose to share it.

To name a trip ("Leeds to York"), the app passes its start and end points to Android's built-in geocoder, which on most phones is a Google service. The trip map loads its map images from OpenStreetMap's tile servers, which see the map area you view and your IP address.

3. Ads

If you use SpeedSight for free, banner ads from Google AdMob appear on the Trips and Settings screens. Ads never appear on the speedometer, the picture-in-picture window or the floating speedometer. AdMob may use your device's advertising ID and information about your device to show and measure ads.

Where the law requires your consent (for example in the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland), the app shows Google's consent form before any ads load, and "Ad privacy choices" in the app's Settings lets you change your answer at any time. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings. SpeedSight Pro has no ads.

4. Purchases

SpeedSight Pro is sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; we never see your card or payment details. The app asks Google Play which purchases your Google account owns, to turn Pro on.

The upgrade screens are provided by Superwall. To show them, Superwall receives app events (such as the app opening and which feature offered Pro), basic device information (model, Android version, language and country) and whether you have Pro, under a random ID that is not linked to your name or email.

5. Crash reports and usage statistics

SpeedSight uses Firebase Crashlytics to send us a report when the app crashes (what went wrong in the code, the phone model and Android version) and Firebase Analytics to count how features are used, such as a trip ending (its length as a range like "5 to 25 km"), a Pro screen being requested, or the floating speedometer being turned on. These reports do not contain your location or your trips, and they use a random ID for this install of the app, not your advertising ID.

6. What we do not collect

  • Your name, email address or phone number (the app has no accounts)
  • Your location history on our servers
  • Contacts, photos, files, call logs or messages
  • Audio: the app never uses the microphone

8. Data retention and deletion

  • Trips stay on your phone until you uninstall the app or clear its storage.
  • Speed-limit answers are cached by rounded location for up to 12 hours, without any link to you.
  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase for 90 days.
  • Because the app has no accounts, we hold no personal profile to delete. For questions about ads data, use "Ad privacy choices" in the app or your Google settings.

9. Children

SpeedSight is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.
