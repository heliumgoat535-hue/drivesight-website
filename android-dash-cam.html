How to Set Up Your Android as a Dash Cam

Takes about 60 seconds. Works on any Android phone made after 2017.

1

Download Phone Dashcam from Google Play

Search "Phone Dashcam" on the Play Store or tap here to install. It's free. No account, no sign-up, no email required.

2

Mount your phone on the windshield

Any windshield suction mount or vent clip holder works. Position it so the rear camera points at the road. Most drivers mount behind the rearview mirror to keep the view clear.

3

Plug into a car charger

Connect the phone to your car's USB charger port. This keeps the phone powered during long drives and ensures parking mode stays active overnight.

4

Tap "Start Drive" — that's it

Open Phone Dashcam and hit Start Drive. Loop recording begins immediately. Crash detection is live. GPS tracking starts. When you park, tap "Park" to switch to parking mode with motion detection.
