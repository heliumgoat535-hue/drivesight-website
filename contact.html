Tips for Faster Support

To help us resolve your issue quickly, include the following in your email when applicable:

  • Device model — e.g., Pixel 7a, Samsung Galaxy S24, etc.
  • Android version — found in Settings > About phone
  • App version — shown in the app's settings screen
  • Steps to reproduce — what you were doing when the issue occurred
  • Screenshots or screen recordings — if the issue is visual

The more context you provide, the faster we can help.
